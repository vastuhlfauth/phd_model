# Scope cube: core indicators across the eight scopes

Generated by `python -m mobgrid.budget.scope_cube`. Weekday described days of ENTD 2008 and EMP 2019, section 9.9's three scope switches (reference `round-dest-local`: Round-trip days · destination purposes · local (≤ 80 km)). Household-bootstrap percentile intervals (200 replicates, one shared set of draws per survey across every scope): 95% for levels, 90% for 2019/2008 ratios, with equivalence verdicts at ±15% and ±10%. Cells of fewer than 50 respondents or trips print their true n with `-` for the values.

Bases: *person-days* is the person-weekday universe (5 × the individual weight), from which the day switch's dropped trip-days are removed, as in section 9.9's open-day rule; *described days* are the kept weekday trip-days, counting zero when every trip of the day is out of scope; *days with trips* keep at least one in-scope trip; per-trip quantities are ratios of weighted totals; *per person* divides the kept days' totals by the person-weekday universe minus the dropped days' weights. Trips per day and the 2019/2008 ratio columns come from the same weighted totals and paired replicates as the CSV. The ENTD 2008 documentation does not guarantee coverage beyond 80 km; observed coverage is similar (Scope section). 2008 `daytable` levels and ratios carry that caveat.
