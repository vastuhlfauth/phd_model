# ENTD 2008 (Enquête nationale transports et déplacements), public tables

- **Source:** SDES, ENTD 2008, national household travel survey, public microdata: https://www.statistiques.developpement-durable.gouv.fr/enquete-nationale-transports-et-deplacements-entd-2008
- **Vintage:** fieldwork April 2007 to April 2008.
- **Local path:** `data/raw/entd2008/` with the four tables below and `documentation.zip` (`Dictionnaire des données.pdf`: code lists; `Liste des variables.pdf`: variables per table).
- **Used by:** step 11 (`mobgrid.budget`), section 9.9's comparison of 2008 and 2019.

| Local file | SDES title | SDES media id |
|---|---|---|
| `k_deploc.csv` | Données mobilité déplacements locaux | 2568 |
| `k_individu.csv` | Données mobilité quotidienne, mobilité du Kish | 2567 |
| `q_tcm_individu.csv` | Données socio-démographiques des individus | 2555 |
| `q_tcm_menage.csv` | Données socio-démographiques des ménages | 2339 |

## Format

- **Encoding:** Windows-1252, re-encoded once to UTF-8 under `data/interim/entd2008/csv/`, as for EMP 2019.
- **Delimiter:** `;`, unquoted, for all four tables; decimal point `.`; missing values are empty strings; dates `dd/mm/yyyy`.
- **Codes are text.** Read every code column (`V2_MTP`, `V2_MMOY*S`, `V2_MMOTIFDES`, `V2_MMOTIFORI`, `V2_TYPJOUR`, `V2_MORIDOM`, `SITUA`, `AGE6`, `TU99`, `v2_moricom_UU2010`, `v2_mdescom_UU2010`) as VARCHAR and map by string: `1.1` (going home) and `1.11` (studying) are different purposes, and two-decimal codes such as `5.50` must not be read as numbers.
- **Header case:** the first column is `idENT_DEP` in `k_deploc` and `idENT_MEN` in `k_individu` and `q_tcm_menage`; DuckDB matches identifiers without regard to case.

## Keys, days and weights

- `IDENT_IND` (person) links `k_deploc`, `k_individu` and `q_tcm_individu`; `IDENT_MEN` (household) links `q_tcm_menage` and is the bootstrap unit.
- **Days.** Each respondent (the Kish individual) describes up to three days: the reference weekday, and Saturday and/or Sunday (both in Île-de-France and Pays de la Loire, one drawn day elsewhere). `IDENT_JOUR` identifies the described day: the day summary groups trips by `IDENT_JOUR`, not by person.
- **Day weight `POIDS_JOUR`:** 5 × `PONDKI` on the reference weekday; 1 × or 2 × `PONDKI` on weekend days; **0 for weekday trips outside the reference day, which are dropped**. `PONDKI` is the person weight.

## Income variables (section 9.9)

Income per consumption unit (modified OECD scale: 1 for the first adult,
0.5 for each other person aged 14 or more, 0.3 for each child under 14),
in quintiles computed within each survey, weighted by the individual
weight (`pond_indC`/`PONDKI`), so that 2008 and 2019 compare positions in
the distribution, not euros (spec 9.9). Quintile cut points are weighted-
cumulative-share thresholds at 20/40/60/80% over the respondent roster
(one row per person, not per trip, so a more mobile respondent does not
weigh more on the cut points); `mobgrid.budget.trip_legs.
weighted_quintile_view_sql` is the shared engine, called once per survey
with its own income value and weight columns.

- **EMP 2019** publishes no raw income bracket or euro value in this
  release ([TO CONFIRM], checked: `K_DEPLOC`, `K_INDIVIDU_public`,
  `Q_MENAGE_public`, `TCM_MEN`'s full dictionary and the real
  `tcm_men.csv` header -- no `revenu`/`tranche` column exists). `TCM_MEN`
  does carry two already-consolidated variables: `COEFFUC` ("somme des
  unités de consommation", INSEE's own modified-OECD consumption-unit
  count, identical formula to the one above) and `decile_rev_uc`
  ("décile de revenu par UC consolidé", INSEE's own, fully imputed
  decile of income per UC -- filled for every respondent, 13,825/13,825
  in the real data, 0% missing). This project uses `decile_rev_uc`
  directly as the continuous income-per-UC rank fed to
  `weighted_quintile_view_sql`, re-cut into quintiles under this
  project's own individual weighting (INSEE's own decile is computed
  under its own, household-based weighting, so the two need not
  coincide exactly at the boundaries). Since `decile_rev_uc` has no
  missing values in the public release, EMP 2019's `'unknown'` income
  class is empty in this data.
- **ENTD 2008** publishes a raw household bracket, `Q_TCM_MENAGE.
  TrancheRevenuMensuel` ("tranche de revenu mensuel net du ménage",
  14 bracket labels, e.g. "De 1 200 € à moins de 1 500 €", "10 000 € ou
  plus"), and `NBUC` ("nombre d'unités de consommation", the
  dictionary's own formula is this project's modified-OECD scale
  verbatim: "+1 pour le premier adulte... +0,5 pour les autres personnes
  de 14 ans ou plus... +0,3 pour les enfants de moins de 14 ans"). This
  project parses the bracket label's bounds (the text before the
  franc-equivalent parenthetical) to a midpoint in euros -- an interior
  bracket's midpoint is the average of its two bounds, the bottom open
  bracket ("Moins de 400 €") at half its upper bound, the top open
  bracket ("10 000 € ou plus") at 1.5 times its lower bound (spec 9.9)
  -- then divides by `NBUC`. `entd2008.income_bracket_midpoint_eur`
  (registered as a DuckDB scalar function) does the parsing; it is
  deliberately not the same as the pre-existing `REVUC`/`NIVIE`/
  `NIVIE10` columns of the same table, which are INSEE's own
  "simulated" income (close but not identical to bracket-midpoint ÷
  `NBUC`: checked on 2,000 real households, only 11.9% match to within
  1 €), kept unused here so the computation matches the spec's own
  bracket-midpoint rule exactly. `TrancheRevenuMensuel` and `NBUC` are
  both filled for every household in the real data (20,178/20,178), so
  ENTD 2008's `'unknown'` income class is also empty in this data; the
  code still handles a missing bracket or a zero/missing `NBUC`
  robustly (labelled `'unknown'`), in case a different release has
  gaps.

## Persons without trips

- 2019: as in step 11 (no trip on the described day).
- 2008, reference weekday: `k_individu.V2_MOBILREF` = 0 (not mobile), weighted by `PONDKI`. Weekend immobility (`V2_MIMMOSAM`, `V2_MIMMODIM`: 1 = immobile) is not needed for the comparison, since EMP 2019 identifies weekday non-travellers only.

## Quirks

- `k_deploc` ends with seven columns that are not in the dictionary: `cpt`, `corr`, `distav`, `distcor`, `vit`, `vmax`, `v2_mduree`. They appear to be SDES's correction of implausible speeds (distance before and after correction, speed, maximum speed, corrected duration). The step reports how many trips have them filled and the mean change, uses `distcor` and `v2_mduree` when filled, and then applies the same upper-bound speed correction as for 2019.
- 2008 splits car drivers into driving alone (3.30) and with passengers (3.31); both are car drivers.
- `V2_MTC1COND` to `V2_MTC4COND` record whether the respondent sat or stood during each public transport leg (1 seated most of the time, 2 about half the time, 3 standing most of the time, 4 standing although a seat was free, 5 not applicable). They are not used in this step, but could calibrate the standing shares of section 9.5 later.
- There is no density grid in ENTD 2008: density classes come from the proxy of section 9.9 in both years for the comparison.
